# Omenabenz Global Ventures Limited

Omenabenz Global Ventures Limited is a Nigerian technology company focused on building software, artificial intelligence systems, cloud infrastructure, APIs, automation platforms, web applications, and digital products.

## What We Build

Our development focus includes:

- **Artificial Intelligence** — AI and machine learning systems for practical applications.
- **Software Applications** — software for web, mobile, and business workflows.
- **Cloud Systems** — cloud services and infrastructure.
- **APIs** — interfaces and integrations that connect applications and services.
- **Automation** — tools that simplify repetitive tasks and workflows.
- **Web Applications** — browser-based applications and digital platforms.
- **Developer Tools** — utilities that support software development.
- **Digital Infrastructure** — systems that support connected digital services.

These areas describe our development focus; they do not imply a released product in every category.
